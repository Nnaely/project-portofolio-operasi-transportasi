# Source attribution

The trip data and taxi zone lookup are published by the New York City Taxi & Limousine Commission (NYC TLC). Yellow taxi records originate from authorized technology providers. NYC TLC does not guarantee their accuracy.

- [Official trip records and monthly download links](https://www.nyc.gov/site/tlc/about/tlc-trip-record-data.page)
- [Yellow Taxi data dictionary, 18 March 2025](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/tlc/downloads/pdf/data_dictionary_trip_records_yellow.pdf)
- [NYC Open Data terms](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/stories/s/Terms-of-Use/k9k7-3cje/)
- [NYC.gov terms](https://www.nyc.gov/home/terms-of-use.page)

The project uses January–April 2025 Yellow Taxi files and the official taxi zone lookup. URLs, retrieval timestamps, byte sizes and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `data/source_manifest.json`. The downloadable portfolio contains aggregate analysis, not the raw trip files. The download script retrieves those files from their official source.
